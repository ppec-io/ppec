About Us

Who we are

A real estate and construction company in Ghana, committed to quality, transparency and customer satisfaction.

Company Overview

Real estate and construction in Ghana

Platinum Properties Estates & Constructions is a real estate and construction company based in Ofinso, in Ghana's Ashanti Region. We help clients buy land and property, develop and build, invest, and manage their assets.

Our work brings property expertise and construction together, so clients can deal with one team from the first conversation through to the finished project.

Company Philosophy

Property and construction, joined up

We believe good property outcomes come from careful planning, honest communication and well-finished work. Bringing real estate and construction together helps keep every stage of a project connected.
