Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Draft. This policy describes how this website works technically. It has not been reviewed by a legal adviser. The company should review and approve it, including its obligations under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), before relying on it.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Platinum Properties Estates & Constructions Company Limited, trading as Platinum Properties Estates & Constructions. You can contact us at info@platinumestatesconstructions.com.

What this website collects

This website has no user accounts, no database and no server of its own. It does not store your enquiries, and it does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Enquiries

The WhatsApp, email and telephone buttons do not send your details to this website. They open your email app, WhatsApp or phone with a message ready to send. If you choose to send it, the message goes to us through that service and is handled under that service's own privacy policy.

If the company switches on the online enquiry form, pressing “Send enquiry” sends your name, contact details, chosen interest and message to us through a third-party form service named next to the form. That service processes the message under its own privacy policy and may store it on its servers, possibly outside Ghana. This website does not keep a copy. We use the details only to reply to your enquiry. [Name the form provider here when it is chosen.]

Job applications

The “Apply” buttons open your email app or WhatsApp. Your CV and details go to us through that service. This website does not receive or store applications.

Maps

Some pages show a Google Maps map. Loading a map connects your browser to Google, which may collect information under Google's own privacy policy.

Information kept in your browser

To remember whether you prefer the grid or list layout while you browse, this website keeps one small setting in your browser's session storage. It is removed when you close the tab.

If you save properties to your shortlist, the list of saved items (their reference numbers only) is kept in your browser's local storage on that device until you remove them or clear your browser data. It is never sent to us or anyone else unless you choose to share it.

The mortgage calculator works entirely in your browser. The figures you type are not stored or sent.

Virtual tours and videos hosted by other services (for example YouTube, Vimeo, Matterport or Kuula) load only after you press play or start. At that point your browser connects to that service, which may collect information under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

If you have sent us an enquiry and want us to correct or delete the details you gave us, please contact us at the email address above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
