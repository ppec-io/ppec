Who we are

Platinum Properties Estates and Constructions Company Limited ("we", "us") operates this website. You can contact us at info@platinumestatesconstructions.com or +233 59 976 2750.

What this website does

This is an information and enquiry website. It does not have user accounts, take payments or take bookings.

Information you give us

If you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp or email, or use the enquiry form, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, phone number, email address and message. We use them to reply to your enquiry.

The enquiry form on this website does not send your message to a server. It opens your own email app or WhatsApp with your message ready to send. Once you send it, your message is handled by those services under their own privacy policies.

Information collected automatically

  • This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • To remember whether you prefer the grid or list layout, the website stores that single choice in your browser's session storage. It is not sent to us, and it is cleared when you close the browser tab.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps. The Contact page shows an embedded Google Map, and property and project pages can show one when you choose “Show map”. Google may collect information, such as your IP address, when a map loads, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply.
